ATK Studios is a technology-focused studio that builds solutions across gaming and entertainment. We started in modding, and our work now spans software, video production and creator management.

The name comes from the **Antikythera mechanism**, the earliest known analog and planetary computer. Its discovery changed what we believed was technologically possible in the ancient world. We take that as our standard: build things that move the field forward.

Our mark is the mechanism's idea in its simplest form. Three tiles, Λ, T and K, fit together into one solid piece.
